Logic Reps Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

The short version

Logic Reps does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no Logic Reps accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Apple's Game Center is optional and stays off until you turn it on.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, solve times, streaks, settings and lesson history are saved in a file inside the app on your iPhone. That file is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

Comparing with friends uses Apple's Game Center, and only if you turn it on inside the app. While it is on, the app sends three kinds of score to Game Center: your times on the day's puzzles when you solve them without a hint, your longest day streak, and how many puzzles you have solved. Game Center shows them under your Game Center name to your friends and, depending on your Game Center settings, to other players. The app also shows you your Game Center friends' scores.

Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The scores go from your device to Apple; we run no server and receive nothing. Turn Game Center off in the app's settings to stop posting. Scores already posted stay with Game Center.

AI coaching

The coach uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. The puzzle positions and questions you give the coach are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us. On devices without Apple Intelligence the coach is simply turned off.

Purchases

Logic Reps Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only learns from the App Store, on your device, whether Pro is unlocked.

Reminders and backups

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device; no server is involved. If you export a backup, it is a file you choose where to save, and it goes nowhere else.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a daily result, the text you choose to share goes only to the app or person you pick in the iOS share sheet.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
