Logic Reps Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version

Logic Reps does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no Logic Reps accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress can sync through your own iCloud account, which only you can read. Apple's Game Center is optional and stays off until you turn it on.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, solve times, streaks, settings and lesson history are saved in a file inside the app on your iPhone or iPad. That file is never sent to us.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your progress (solves and times, streak, rest days, ratings, points, quests, lessons and unfinished boards, but not your settings) is kept in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, so your other iPhones and iPads with Logic Reps can share it and reinstalling the app brings it back. It is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Only your Apple Account can read it: we can't see it, and nothing passes through a server of ours. Turn "Sync with iCloud" off in the app's settings to keep progress on one device.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my data. After two confirmations it erases your progress from that device and from iCloud, and every other device that syncs with your iCloud erases its copy the next time it opens the app. It can't be undone, so the app offers to export a backup first. You can also remove the iCloud copy in the Settings app under your name, iCloud, Manage Storage (or Storage), Logic Reps; deleting the app removes the copy on that device. Scores already posted to Game Center stay with Game Center, and Logic Reps Pro stays with your Apple Account.

Game Center

Comparing with friends uses Apple's Game Center, and only if you turn it on inside the app. While it is on, the app sends four kinds of score to Game Center: your times on the day's puzzles when you solve them without a hint, your longest day streak, how many puzzles you have solved, and your points for the week. It also reports the achievements you have earned. Game Center shows them under your Game Center name to your friends and, depending on your Game Center settings, to other players. The app also shows you your Game Center friends' scores.

With Game Center on, the Friends screen can also show Everyone: the day's quickest times worldwide, read from Game Center's own leaderboards, with other players' Game Center names. The Friends widget keeps your friends' Game Center names and weekly points on your device, in the app's own storage, so the widget can show them; they go nowhere else.

Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The scores go from your device to Apple; we run no server and receive nothing. Turn Game Center off in the app's settings to stop posting. Scores already posted stay with Game Center.

AI coaching

The coach uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. The puzzle positions and questions you give the coach are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us. On devices without Apple Intelligence the coach is simply turned off.

Purchases

Logic Reps Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only learns from the App Store, on your device, whether Pro is unlocked.

Reminders and backups

The optional daily reminder and streak reminder are scheduled on your device from what is already stored there; no server is involved. If you export a backup, it is a file you choose where to save, and it goes nowhere else.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a daily result, a streak or a challenge, or Invite a friend, the text you choose to share goes only to the app or person you pick in the iOS share sheet. A challenge link holds the game, the level, a board number and your time, nothing about you.

The Logic Reps App Clip, which opens a shared board for someone who doesn't have the app, works the same way as the app: everything stays on the device, and it sends nothing.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Racing in Messages

The Logic Reps app in Messages (iPhone and iPad) sends a race as an ordinary iMessage message to the people in that chat: the board's number, and each player's time, mistakes and hints. Players are told apart by the random IDs Messages gives each person in a chat; the app never reads names or phone numbers. So that the race board looks like yours, the Messages app reads a copy of your settings, board theme and today's results that the Logic Reps app keeps in its own storage on your device. A race you finish is passed back to the app on the same device. Nothing goes to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
