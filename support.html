Common questions

Is every puzzle solvable without guessing?

Yes. Each board has exactly one answer and is checked by a solver before you see it. If you are stuck, tap Hint for the next logical step and the reason for it.

Why does the coach say it is unavailable?

The coach uses Apple Intelligence. It needs a supported iPhone on iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Everything else in the app works without it.

What is free, and what is Logic Reps Pro?

The five games, today's five, hints and all the lessons are free, with no limit on how much you play. Pro adds a workout every day, drills for every technique, boards built around a technique you choose, the skill map, pace tracking and past days of the daily five. Your first three workouts are free.

I bought Pro but it is locked

Open the Play tab, tap the gear, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Play tab, tap the gear, then Manage subscription. You can also cancel in the Settings app under your name, then Subscriptions. Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I compare times with friends?

Open the Play tab and tap Friends on the Today's five card, then turn on Game Center. You will see your Game Center friends' times on today's five boards, along with streaks and puzzles solved. Tap Add friends to invite someone. Game Center is optional, and you can turn it off again in the app's settings.

Why isn't my time on the friends board?

A time is posted only when you solve one of today's five without a hint, and only while Game Center is turned on and signed in. Each board lists times for today's puzzle only, so a friend in a far-off time zone can be missing for part of the day.

How do I reset my progress?

Open the Play tab, tap the gear, then Reset all progress.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress is stored on your device and is included in your normal iPhone backup. It is not synced between devices.
