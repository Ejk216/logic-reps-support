Common questions

Is every puzzle solvable without guessing?

Yes. Each board has exactly one answer and is checked by a solver before you see it. If you are stuck, tap Hint for the next logical step and the reason for it. Hints fit how you play: for something you have solved many times on your own, a hint first lights up only the area, and Tell me more gives the full reason (still one hint); while you are new to the app, or still learning a technique, you get the full reason straight away. It all works from your record on your device. After a hint, the next one is ready after 30 seconds of play; the button counts down, and a hint never adds time to your clock.

Why don't I see the coach?

The coach answers questions in lessons and in the trainer, using Apple Intelligence on your device. It shows only where Apple Intelligence is available: a supported iPhone, iPad or Mac on iOS 26, iPadOS 26 or macOS 26 or later, with it turned on in Settings. Lessons, hints, drills and the trainer's workouts all work without it, and every technique lesson has its own tips on where to look first and the common mistakes.

What is free, and what is Logic Reps Pro?

Every game, today's six, hints and all the lessons are free, with no limit on how much you play. Pro adds a workout every day, drills for every technique, boards built around a technique you choose, the skill map, pace tracking, each game's rating history, past days of the daily six, the trainer's notes in your weekly recap, four more board themes and four more app icons. Your first three workouts are free, and the yearly plan starts with a free week.

I bought Pro but it is locked

Open the Play tab, tap the gear, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Play tab, tap the gear, then Manage subscription. You can also cancel in the Settings app under your name, then Subscriptions. Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free week, you aren't charged and Pro stays on until the week ends. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I compare times with friends?

Open the Friends tab. Add friends in Logic Reps with a link, a QR code or AirDrop (it uses iCloud, not Game Center). If you both have Logic Reps, one of you opens Add a friend and the other taps Scan a friend's code there and points the camera at it, without leaving the app, and you see each other's times on today's six and your day streaks under Your friends. Or, under Game Center, turn on Game Center to see your Game Center friends' times on today's six boards, along with streaks, puzzles solved and points this week. Tap Add friends there to send a Game Center friend request, or switch to Everyone to see the day's quickest times worldwide. Game Center is optional, and you can turn it off again in the app's settings. A Game Center friend shows up once they've turned on Game Center in Logic Reps and posted a time that day.

How do I challenge a friend?

After you solve a board, tap Challenge a friend to send it with your time to beat. A friend who doesn't have Logic Reps can play the board straight away in the Logic Reps App Clip, with nothing to install. To invite someone to today's six, open the Friends tab and tap Invite a friend: send the link with AirDrop or Messages, or let them scan the QR code. Next to them? Tap Send, then hold the tops of your iPhones together (iOS 17 or later on both); they tap Accept and open the link. If AirDrop doesn't find them, the QR code always works. If they already have Logic Reps, they can tap Scan a friend's code on their own Invite a friend screen and scan yours. You can also send any board you solved today from Challenge a friend in the Friends tab.

How do I add a friend in Logic Reps?

Open the Friends tab and tap Add a friend under Your friends (or Invite a friend with Add them as a friend on), then send the link, show the code, or hold your iPhones together. When they open it and tap Add, you both see each other's times on today's six and your day streaks under Your friends. It uses iCloud, not Game Center, so you both need to be signed in to iCloud. Tap a friend to rename them on your phone or to remove them; removing works both ways, and they stop seeing your times too.

What do I get for inviting friends?

Items, free and never sold: a friend new to Logic Reps gets the Side by side trail as a welcome gift when they open your invite. You earn items as friends join through your invite: at 1 friend the Side by side trail and the Good company title, at 3 the Lanyard for Larry and the Pen pal badge frame, at 5 the Gingham board theme and a backpack for Larry, and at 10 the Postcard board theme. A friend counts when they finish a first board with your link, play a race with you in Messages, or send you their time. Sharing the link doesn't count by itself, each friend counts once, and none of this is part of Logic Reps Pro or needs a rating or review. Your Collection shows them under Bring friends.

I invited a friend and nothing counted.

A friend counts once they finish a first board with your link, and your phone needs to be online and signed in to iCloud to hear about it; open Logic Reps and look at the Friends tab. A friend who already played Logic Reps before opening the link doesn't count, and neither does one of your own devices.

What is "Add a Game Center friend?"

After a first board with a friend's invite, Logic Reps offers to open Apple's Game Center friend request so you can see each other's times. It is optional; "Not now" ends it.

I turned down Game Center's sign-in. How do I turn it on?

Apple shows its Game Center sign-in to each app only once. In the Settings app, go back to the main list, tap Game Center and sign in, then come back to Logic Reps; it notices by itself. Or, in the Friends tab, tap Turn off Game Center in Logic Reps to go back to the plain offer.

Why isn't my time on the friends board?

Your time on each of today's six is posted when you solve it, while Game Center is turned on and signed in. A hint never keeps a time off the boards, and it never adds time to your clock: a time that took a hint is posted like any other and shows how many beside it. The same goes for a race in Messages. Each board lists times for today's puzzle only, so a friend in a far-off time zone can be missing for part of the day.

What notifications does Logic Reps send?

Only the reminders you turn on, both off at first. Open the Play tab and tap the gear to find them. The daily reminder comes once a day at the time you pick, and is skipped once that day's six are solved. The streak reminder comes at 8 PM on a day you haven't played yet, when your streak is 2 days or more and no rest day is left to cover it. Both are scheduled on your device, and tapping one opens today's six.

Why are today's six on my Lock Screen?

After you solve your first daily of the day, Logic Reps keeps today's six on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity, with how many are done and your streak. It updates as you solve them, shows "All six done" for a few minutes once you finish, and goes away at midnight. If you swipe it away, it stays away until tomorrow. To stop it, open the Play tab, tap the gear and turn off Today's six on the Lock Screen. It runs entirely on your device and sends nothing.

How are weekly points counted?

Every board you solve earns points: 10 on Easy, 20 on Medium and 30 on Hard, 10 more when you use no hints and make no wrong moves, and 5 more when you beat your average for that game and level. The week runs Monday to Sunday in your own time zone, and three new quests start each Monday. You'll find both in the Progress tab. On your first visit of a new week, a short recap of the last one opens; you can see it again any time under Last week in the Progress tab.

How do levels work?

Every point you earn counts toward your level, for good. Level 2 takes 250 points, and each level after that takes 100 more than the one before, so the first few come within a week or two of daily play and the later ones take months. Some levels bring a new title, shown under your level in the Progress tab, and levels 4, 8 and 12 bring a new board theme. Spending points never lowers your level.

How do I change how the boards look?

Open Settings (the gear on the Play tab) or the Progress tab and tap Board theme. Paper is the default. Graph, Chalk and Clay are free and unlock at levels 4, 8 and 12. Harbor, Press, Dusk and Lacquer are part of Logic Reps Pro; without Pro you can tap one to see it on sample boards in Light and Dark Mode first. A theme changes the colours of every board and the names hints use for Stars regions, never the puzzles. Each theme follows Light and Dark Mode, except Chalk and Lacquer, which look the same in both. If a Pro subscription ends, the boards go back to Paper, and your theme returns if you subscribe again. With Pro you can also pick one of four other app icons in Settings under App icon.

Can I get a rest day with points?

Yes. While you hold fewer than two rest days, open the Progress tab and tap Get a rest day. It costs 300 points, and the app asks before spending them. You still earn rest days for every 7 days in a row you play, and one is used for you when you miss a day. Spending points doesn't lower your level or your points for the week, so the weekly board with friends isn't affected. Points come only from solving boards: they can't be bought, and they don't unlock Logic Reps Pro.

How does the skill rating work?

Each game has its own rating, starting at 1,000. Every daily, practice, past-day and challenge board you solve moves it, like a puzzle rating in chess: a board counts as tougher at Medium and Hard, and a quicker solve than your usual time at that level counts for more. A board with no hints and no wrong moves never lowers it, and a board where you needed hints lowers it by a few points at most. Trainer boards and drills don't count, so practising a weak spot never costs you. If you played before ratings arrived, your rating starts from the boards you had already solved. You'll find it after every solve and in the Progress tab. With Logic Reps Pro, the Progress tab also charts how each game's rating has moved over your latest boards.

What does "Faster than 72% of players today" mean?

With Game Center on, after you solve one of today's six, Logic Reps asks Game Center where your time stands among everyone who posted one on that game's board today. It only shows when at least 20 players have posted, so the number means something.

Does my progress sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes, through your own iCloud account, with nothing to sign up for. Use the same Apple Account on each and your streak, rest days, ratings, points, quests, times and unfinished boards are the same on all of them. Play on two of them on the same day, or on one while another is offline, and nothing is lost or counted twice once they meet. Your settings, like reminders, stay on each device. If you delete the app and install it again, your progress comes back from iCloud. You can turn sync off in Settings, under Progress & iCloud, which also says whether this device is up to date. If iCloud is off on a device, progress stays on that device.

How do I delete my data?

Open the Play tab, tap the gear, then Delete my data. The app first lists exactly what will go, with your own numbers, and offers to export a backup. A second confirmation, Delete everything, then erases your progress from this device and from iCloud; your other devices erase theirs the next time they open the app. It can't be undone. It also makes a new invite code, and removes you from every friend you added in Logic Reps. Logic Reps Pro stays with your Apple Account (tap Restore purchases), and scores already in Game Center stay there. If you shared usage summaries, sharing is turned off on this device and the summaries it sent are deleted, the next time it is online and signed in to the same iCloud account. Your other devices do the same when they erase their progress; a device with iCloud sync off doesn't hear about the delete, so turn sharing off there yourself.

Does Logic Reps collect anything about how I play?

Only if you say yes (from version 1.4). Usage sharing is off until you turn it on, on the card the app shows once after a solve or in Settings under Privacy. If you turn it on, about once a week that device saves a short summary of how you played to Logic Reps' own iCloud database: rough time in each game and screen, boards started, finished and left, hints by technique, and walkthroughs finished or skipped. It holds no name, email, board numbers, solve times or times of day. Apple stamps each summary with the time it was saved and an ID for your iCloud account in this app, which the developer can see. Turning the switch off, or Delete my data, deletes the summaries that device sent, the next time it is online and signed in to the same iCloud account. The privacy policy lists everything a summary holds.

What is Woodcut?

A picture puzzle, new in 1.3. The numbers beside each row and column give its runs of filled cells, in order. Fill the cells and cross off the rest until every line matches, and a small picture appears. Every board has one answer and never needs a guess. A Woodcut board is Today's warm-up on Home; it doesn't count toward today's six or your streak.

How do I learn a game?

The first time you open a game, a short walkthrough on a small board shows each rule as you use it. You can skip it, and replay it any time from How to play in the game's menu. When a move breaks a rule, a line under the board says which one.

What is Ease me in?

An easier start for players new to puzzles like these: for your first week, each game offers a short warm-up board before the day's board, and the weekend's harder boards come with an easy one first. Choose it when the app first asks, or turn it on in Settings. It's never required.

What is the Collection?

Things you earn by playing: accessories for Larry, titles, level badges and frames, board themes and the pictures you finish in Woodcut. You earn them at every level and for streaks, weekly quests, lessons, challenges and milestones in each game. Tap any item to try it on before you earn it. Logic Reps Pro adds piece styles, trails, solve effects and a few more items.

What is First Snow?

A winter event from December 1 to 24: one board a day, with a hat and scarf for Larry and the Frostlight board theme to earn along the way. If you miss a day, you can catch up on it before the event ends.

How do I race a friend in Messages?

On iPhone or iPad, open a chat in Messages, tap the apps button, choose Logic Reps and pick a board. Everyone in the chat plays the same board, and the bubble shows each person's time and who won, with a rematch a tap away. Your race counts toward your points and streak in the app.

How do I race on one device?

On iPhone Duo opened flat or on iPad, tap Race a friend in the Friends tab. You each take a side of the same board, and the first correct solve wins. These races don't earn points.

How do I show someone the app?

Open the Friends tab and tap Invite a friend. A friend points their camera at the code and plays today's six straight away, with nothing to install. A friend who has Logic Reps can scan it from their own Invite a friend screen with Scan a friend's code; the first time, Logic Reps asks to use the camera.

Can points keep my streak?

Yes, if you turn on Use points to keep my streak in Settings. When you miss a day and have no rest day left, 300 points buy one for you, as long as you have enough for every day you missed. Spending points never lowers your level or your week's points.

Can I move the controls to the other side?

On a wide screen, such as iPhone Duo opened flat, iPad or Mac, a game's buttons sit beside the board. Choose right or left in Settings.

Why is the board blank in my screenshot?

While you're playing, boards are left out of screenshots and screen recordings, so a paused board can't be solved from a photo. Finished boards, results and the rest of the app capture normally. On a Mac the whole window is left out while a board is in play.

Why does Logic Reps say a new version is out?

From 1.4, when the app opens it asks the App Store which version is current. If yours is older, the Play tab offers "Update", which opens the App Store page, or "Not now", which hides the offer for a week (a newer version brings it back sooner). It never appears over a board, a lesson or a race. The request names the app and your country setting and nothing about you, and offline it does nothing. To stop seeing it, update; the update is free and your progress comes with it.

Is Logic Reps on the Mac?

Yes, from 1.3. It's the same app, so Logic Reps Pro bought on your iPhone or iPad works on your Mac too, and your progress syncs through iCloud. Play with the mouse, trackpad, keyboard or touch.

Is my progress backed up?

With iCloud sync on, your progress is kept in your iCloud as well as on your device. You can also export a backup file in Settings and import it later; while sync is on, importing adds the backup to your progress instead of replacing it.
